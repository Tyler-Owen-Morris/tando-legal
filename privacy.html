AllHands Household Alerts: Privacy Policy

Tando Productions LLC · Effective October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information AllHands Household Alerts collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. AllHands is a private household system operated by Tando Productions LLC ("we", "us"). The alerts are sent only to adult members of the household that runs it.

1. What we collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number, given to the account holder when you sign up.
  • The record of your consent: your answer and the date.
  • The alerts sent to you (text and time), whether they were delivered, and your replies, including HELP, STOP and START.
  • For phone call alerts: the time of the call, whether it was answered, and any key you pressed to confirm it.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to send you household alerts, to know whether an alert reached you, to honor your choice to stop, and to keep the records that mobile carriers require. We do not use it for marketing or advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers that deliver the messages and calls on our behalf (our messaging provider, Twilio, and the mobile carriers), which may use it only for that purpose.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Where it is kept, and for how long

Your number, consent record and alert history are kept on the household's own AllHands system, which is not open to the public internet. Our messaging provider keeps its own delivery records under its own policies. We keep your information for as long as you are signed up. If you stop the alerts, we keep only the record that you opted out, so we do not message you again.

5. Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop the alerts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Ask the account holder, or write to tylerowenmorris@gmail.com, to see, correct or delete the information held about you.

6. Children

The alerts are for adults. We do not knowingly collect mobile numbers from children under 13 or send them messages.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
