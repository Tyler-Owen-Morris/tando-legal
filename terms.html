AllHands Household Alerts: Terms of Service

Tando Productions LLC · Effective October 10, 2026

These terms cover the text message and phone call alerts ("AllHands Household Alerts") sent by AllHands, a private household system operated by Tando Productions LLC ("we", "us"). By agreeing to receive the alerts you accept these terms.

1. What the program is

AllHands is a home hub used by one household. AllHands Household Alerts sends text messages, and when needed places an automated phone call, to adult members of that household about urgent things at home that need attention. Examples: an appliance alert such as the washer finishing or a machine needing attention, an urgent family reminder, or a notice that the home system has a problem. An alert is sent by text or call only when the notification in the AllHands app has not been answered.

We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

2. Who can sign up, and how

The program is open only to adult members of the household that runs AllHands. You sign up by giving your consent in person to the account holder, who reads you the consent script and records your answer. Consent is not a condition of buying anything or of using AllHands.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies with what happens at home. You will receive no more than 5 messages a day.

4. Cost

We do not charge for the alerts. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

5. Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or write to tylerowenmorris@gmail.com.

6. Stopping

Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving alerts. You will get one message confirming that you have been removed, and no more after that. Reply START to sign up again.

7. Delivery

Alerts are sent through mobile carriers and may be delayed or not delivered. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. The alerts are a convenience and are not an emergency service: do not rely on them in place of smoke alarms, security systems, or calling emergency services.

8. Your number

Tell the account holder if your mobile number changes or you give up the number, so alerts are not sent to someone else.

9. Privacy

How we handle your mobile number and messages is described in our Privacy Policy.
